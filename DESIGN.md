## Overview

Industrial precision workbench aesthetic on warm stone paper (`#F5F6F4`) with slate-navy primary actions (`#274C69`), high-contrast dark amber warnings (`#9A4F00`), and crisp vector surfaces (`#FFFFFF`). Designed for print-production operators preparing physical 70 × 70 mm QR plaques.

## Style Foundations

- **Visual style:** industrial workbench, functional, high-contrast, print-ready
- **Typography scale:** 12/14/16/18/20/24/28/30/34
- **Typography fonts:** primary=Gotham (`--font-ui`), display=Gotham (`--font-ui`), mono=ui-monospace
- **Typography weights:** 300, 400, 500, 600, 700
- **Color palette:** primary (`#274C69`), secondary (`#8A5A19`), surface (`#FFFFFF`), background (`#F5F6F4`), ink (`#000000`), success (`#16A34A`), warning (`#9A4F00`), danger (`#DC2626`)
- **Spacing scale:** 4/8/12/16/24/32

## Colors

- **Primary (#274C69):** Deep slate blue for primary CTAs, active step indicators, and focus rings.
- **Secondary (#8A5A19):** Warm bronze accent for secondary interactive states.
- **Success (#16A34A):** Ready-to-print status and validated rows.
- **Warning (#9A4F00):** High-contrast dark amber (WCAG AA ≥ 4.5:1 on light surfaces) for pending QR generation and duplicate warnings.
- **Danger (#DC2626):** Blocking errors, destructive actions, and print-bleed/quiet-zone violations.
- **Background (#F5F6F4):** Warm stone workspace canvas that separates white cards and physical piece previews.
- **Surface (#FFFFFF):** Clean card, modal, and plaque preview surface.
- **Text (#111827) & Ink (#000000):** Primary UI text and default vector QR ink.
